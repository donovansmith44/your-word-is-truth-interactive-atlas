## NAMES: one registry for text and maps
The owner's rulings of 09-29, held in the queue item `A-NAMES`:
- **A name is its own node, separate from what it names.** One name can mean entities of different kinds: "Israel" is Jacob (a person), the Israelites (a people), the kingdom (a polity) and the land (a place). Entities that share a name are linked to each other.
- **Every occurrence resolves to exactly one meaning,** and anchors to the specific original-language word it translates, not only that word's Strong's number, so it resolves the same way in any translation.
- **Verses and words do not line up across texts.**
  - Each text keeps its own verse numbering, and moving between numberings goes through an explicit map.
  - Each translation reaches the original words only through its own alignment table. With no alignment, the occurrence stays unresolved and is listed, never inferred from position.
  - The brain-fuel/STEPBible data comes pre-renumbered to KJV verses. That is recorded as provenance, not relied on silently.
- **Titles are not names, but they are mapped and explorable** ("the king of Assyria", "the Preacher", "the Son of man"): a title layer resolves each occurrence to the entity it refers to. Theologically loaded titles go on an owner review list before they're served.
- **Pronouns and coreference are out of scope.** Multi-word and hyphenated names (Kirjath-arba, Beth-el) are one name over several words.
- **Resolution is by rules, plus curation for what the rules refuse.** Never guessed.
- **Sources** (licenses checked 2026-09-29, `.superpowers/analysis/name-sources.md`): BibleForgeDB (PD/CC0) for KJV word → original token; STEPBible TIPNR (CC BY) for the entity per original token; STEPBible TVTMS (CC BY) for versification maps; BibleData (CC BY) for titles. Our KJV differs from BibleForge's edition in 452 verses, so the join is explicit.

**Map-generator's entity registry is the same problem.** It already works as "one identity per real thing, unified by written reason, never by slug". So the map migration's hardest part, matching its places and polities to the atlas's, is designed here, once. The MAPS spec builds on NAMES.

## Map correctness
The owner reports many apparent errors in map-generator's output.
- **The register:** errors are found by rendering every era map and auditing it against Scripture and the atlas's own facts, recorded in map-generator's `docs/errata/`.
- **Rulings:** the owner rules on each entry.
- **Fixes:** each fix is written test-first, as data with its justification (map-generator's covenant: Bible-driven authority, no tuned constants). A golden view is re-blessed only with the owner's approval.
- **Timing:** the register moves into the atlas with the migration. Fixing pauses during the move itself.
